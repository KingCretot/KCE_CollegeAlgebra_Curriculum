Chapter 5

Systems & Matrices

Eight lessons matching Sections 5.1–5.8 of Essentials of College Algebra, 12th ed. (Lial, Hornsby, Schneider, Daniels): solving linear systems by substitution, elimination, matrices, and determinants; partial fractions; nonlinear systems; systems of inequalities and linear programming; matrix operations; and matrix inverses.
