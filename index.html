KCE College Algebra

Interactive lessons that follow Essentials of College Algebra, 12th edition (Lial, Hornsby, Schneider, Daniels; Pearson), section by section, for students taking MAC 1105 College Algebra. Each lesson opens with the big idea, then works through examples, guided practice, and a quick quiz; each chapter ends with a review and practice test.
